# Space imagery credits

`hubble-atlas.webp` packs 16 galaxies and 3 globular clusters (cells left to right, top to bottom, in the order below)
into one image for the home-page starfield (`src/scripts/starfield.ts`). Each was cropped square, its sky level
set to black and its edges feathered, then scaled to a 192 px cell.

Images: NASA, ESA and the Hubble Space Telescope, from the NASA Image and Video Library. NASA imagery is not
copyrighted; ESA/Hubble imagery is CC BY 4.0, which this credit and the footer line satisfy.
