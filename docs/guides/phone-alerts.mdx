A notification is easy to miss. With phone alerts, GAIA texts or rings your phone when something
you care about happens:

- a scheduled job fails
- a task has waited on your approval too long
- GAIA's model server can't start
- an agent you allowed to reach you needs you

The call says what kind of thing happened and asks you to open GAIA. It never reads out your
files, email or anything a task or agent wrote.

## What leaves your PC

A call or text crosses the phone network, so the provider sees your number, GAIA's number, the
time and a fixed sentence such as "This is GAIA on your PC. A scheduled job failed. Open GAIA to
see it." Nothing else leaves: no file names, email subjects, task output or agent text. The
models stay on your PC.

## Try it without an account

Settings › Phone starts on the **Simulated phone**. Calls and texts are recorded and shown in
Settings instead of being sent, so you can set up the phone book, rules and limits and send a
test alert before signing up anywhere.

## Set up Twilio

GAIA uses [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com) for real calls and texts. An individual can sign up in
a few minutes, and the trial's free credit covers testing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    Create an account at [twilio.com/try-twilio](https://www.twilio.com/try-twilio) and verify
    your own phone number when it asks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a number">
    In the Twilio console, choose **Get a phone number**. This is the number GAIA calls from.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your keys">
    On the console's home page, copy the **Account SID** (it starts with `AC`) and the **Auth
    Token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add them to GAIA">
    In Settings › Phone, choose **Twilio**, paste the Account SID, Auth Token and your Twilio
    number, and save. GAIA checks them against your account right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your number">
    Enter your own number. GAIA calls it and says a 6-digit code; type it in within 10 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn phone on and test">
    Turn phone on, then choose **Send test alert**. Your phone rings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Use a Twilio account (or subaccount) just for GAIA, keep its balance low and turn off
  auto-recharge. In Twilio's **Voice Geographic Permissions**, allow only your own country.
  Anyone with the Auth Token can use the account, and GAIA can't limit what it allows.
</Warning>

**On a trial account,** Twilio plays a short trial notice before GAIA speaks and asks you to press
a key. A trial can only call numbers verified in the Twilio console, so verify each number there
as well as in GAIA. Upgrading the account removes both limits.

**Texts in the US** need the number registered with carriers first: toll-free verification for a
toll-free number, or 10DLC registration for a local one. Twilio advises against starting
toll-free verification on a trial. Calls work in the meantime; turn texts on in Settings once
your number is approved.

## The phone book

GAIA only calls or texts numbers in your phone book.

- A number gets in only when you type the code that reached that phone.
- Adding a second number also needs a code sent to the first one, so someone holding only the new
  phone can't add it.
- Every addition or removal is announced by a call to the numbers already there.
- Removing a number needs a code sent to it. To remove the last one, turn phone off first.
- GAIA refuses emergency, short, premium-rate and satellite numbers.

## How an alert escalates

Each rule picks its steps: notify only, notify and text, or notify, text and call.

1. The alert shows in the Agent UI and the terminal app.
2. A text after 5 minutes if nobody opened it.
3. A call 5 minutes after that. Rules marked urgent text and call right away.
4. If voicemail or nobody answers, GAIA calls again two minutes later, then tries your next number.

Opening or dismissing the alert in either app stops every step left. A call a person answers
stops further calls for that alert. Voicemail doesn't count as answered.

**Limits:** 6 calls and 20 texts a day by default, reset at local midnight. Alerts raised by an
agent have their own limit of 2 calls a day, so they can't use up the calls a real failure needs.
Repeats of the same alert within 30 minutes join the open one.

**Quiet hours** hold every alert except rules marked urgent until they end.

**Make it ring through Do Not Disturb:** save GAIA's Twilio number as a contact. On iPhone, turn on
Emergency Bypass for it, and turn on Settings › Focus › Allow repeated calls. On Android, star the
contact and allow starred contacts through Do Not Disturb.

## Talking with GAIA on a call

With mobile access on and a PIN set, a call from GAIA doesn't end after the
alert. It connects to your PC:

1. GAIA says the alert, then asks for your PIN. Enter it on the keypad and press **#**.
2. Talk normally. GAIA answers in her own voice (Settings › Phone › Voice), from
   your PC: the same agent, tools and memory as a chat.
3. When something needs your OK, she says what she wants to do. Say **yes** or
   **no**, or press **1** to allow or **2** to deny.
4. Say **bye**, or hang up. The call is saved as a chat named "Phone call".

Nothing is said and nothing runs before the right PIN. Three wrong PINs end the
call. Without mobile access, calls say the alert sentence and hang up.

## Calling GAIA

Call GAIA's Twilio number from a number in your phone book. She asks for your
PIN, then it's the same conversation. GAIA points the Twilio number at your PC
whenever mobile access is on, so there's nothing to set in the Twilio console.
Calls from any other number are rejected, and a caller ID that looks like
yours still needs the PIN.

## Letting an agent alert you

Turn on **Agents may alert me** to give the GAIA agent an `alert_owner` tool. The agent can then
ask GAIA to reach you; your rules still decide whether that's a text or a call, and you hear the
fixed sentence, not the agent's words. The agent's reason shows in the apps. Start a new chat after
changing this setting.

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Twilio rejected the Account SID or Auth Token | Copy both again from the Twilio console. |
| Verify this number in the Twilio console too | The account is a trial; verify the number there or upgrade. |
| Not called: daily limit reached | The day's call limit is used up; raise it in Settings › Phone. |
| Held for quiet hours | The alert waits for quiet hours to end. |
| Calls only while this PC is awake | A sleeping PC can't call. Keep it plugged in and awake. |
