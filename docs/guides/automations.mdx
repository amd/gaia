Ask GAIA in plain words to do something later, again and again, or only when
something changes. It sets it up after you approve it, and messages you when
there is something to say.

<Note>
Automations reach you through [Discord](/guides/discord) today. Connect it
under Settings → Messaging in the GAIA app first; GAIA tells you if nothing
would deliver what you set up.
</Note>

## What you can ask for

| You say | GAIA sets up | It messages you |
|---|---|---|
| "Remind me in 10 minutes to stretch", "remind me at 5pm to call Sam" | a **reminder** | once, at that time |
| "Summarize my inbox every morning" | a **summary** | every time |
| "Alert me if a test breaks on main", "tell me when PR 12 is approved" | a **trigger** | only when the thing it watches changes |
| "Let me know when there's a good weekend to climb Rainier", "tell me if anything urgent lands in my inbox" | an **alert** | only when it finds something that needs you, and never twice about the same thing |
| "Check in on things every 30 minutes, but not at night" | a **heartbeat** | only when something on your checklist needs you |

- **Triggers cost almost nothing.** A small read-only check runs on your PC every few
  minutes without the AI; GAIA wakes only when the result changes. Checks can only
  read from GitHub today, so they cannot change anything.
- **Alerts and the heartbeat use judgment.** Every interval GAIA looks (the web, your
  mail, a forecast) and decides whether it is worth telling you. Quiet runs are not
  shown at all.
- **Quiet hours** hold a run until they end, so a heartbeat never wakes you at 3 a.m.

## Seeing and changing them

Ask: "what are you watching?", "list my reminders", "pause the heartbeat", "check main
every 30 minutes instead", "stop the Rainier alert". GAIA lists them by name and changes
or removes the one you mean, after you approve the change.

From a terminal, the same thing is [`gaia automation`](/reference/cli#automation):

```bash
gaia automation list --plain
gaia automation pause "Heartbeat"
gaia automation remove "Stretch"
```

## Limits

- At most 25 automations, and repeating ones run at most every 5 minutes.
- Nothing runs while the PC is off or the Discord bridge is stopped. A repeating one
  that came due meanwhile runs once when the bridge is back, not once per missed slot.
- A run that needs a change on your PC still asks first, in Discord, like any other.
  Reading its own list or a branch's CI status does not.
